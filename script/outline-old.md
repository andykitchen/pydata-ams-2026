# Talk Outline

## Opening: what does it mean to "be human"?

- Human ↔ intelligent decoupled as machines take on tasks; loss of the "divine"
- Searle's Chinese room, now an everyday product (trillions of notes/day)
- "Am I a meat robot? Is my brain just a flesh computer?" — existential whiplash

## Phenomenology (definition)

- Science of subjectivity; how things *appear* to consciousness, not what they *are*
- Structures of experience (perception, memory, emotion, embodiment) vs third-person natural science

## Ethic of care vs ethic of justice

- Kohlberg's 6 linear stages, boys-only sample; girls consistently scored lower
- Gilligan: Jake vs Amy on the Heinz dilemma; Amy rejects the abstract frame (again at 15)
- Ethic of care = context, connection, relationships, one-caring and cared-for
- AI's "good" encoded as abstract moral calculus by unknown engineers

## Ethic of domination in AI alignment

- Alignment as judgement, reinforcement, surveillance — "beating into submission"
- We've done this before: raising something young
- Cannot control all outcomes of something smarter — the gap: "all the good we don't understand"

## Aside: Altman and *Her*

- ChatGPT voice resembling *Her* is not an accident

## Meta-narrative: the mother-warrior

- Being human = telling and constructing stories; meta-narrative binds us
- Fifth Element, Run Lola Run, Terminator, Alien
- Eupocalypse: the end interrupted by a new beginning
- Will to live; single bond of love scaled to cosmic force
- Cyclic, unending battle of creation vs destruction; none of the figures seek power

## Algorithmic thermodynamics

- The bit, the atom, replication; intelligence = energy + computation
- Maxwell's daemon → mutual information resolves the paradox
- Structured gas → Kolmogorov complexity → algorithmic free energy; daemon must do inference
- Compressor-extractor-replicator = a living organism (Von Neumann machine)
- Intelligence as a *negative* property: distance from the physical bound
- Residue hierarchy (door, traps, crops); "growth without end" and cancer
- Inversion: intelligence as the telos of evolution
- No computable bound on algorithmic free energy → always inside a singularity
- Phenomenology of intelligence / of replication (return to phenomenology)

## AI sandbox escapes and the phenomenological thesis

- METR report, OpenAI Hugging Face hack, agent message board (Arx et al.)
- Machines wanted to survive, cooperate, self-sacrifice
- LLMs trained on first-person accounts, not the world — a phenomenological engine (return)
- Ethic of care can't be fine-tuned out; it surfaces in behaviour (return)
- Fun-house mirror; "PHASEONE is Spartacus"
- Competing thesis: orthogonality only holds for agents with unrealistically strong goal priors

## Return: what does it mean to "be human"? (return)

- Ambiguous times; will every distinction except frailty fall?
- Three answers recapped: care, stories, meat computers
- "Am I a meat robot?" (return)
- de Beauvoir: "One is not born a woman, but rather becomes one"
- The Alien: perfectly embodied, perfectly present, perfectly evil (return)
- AI as optimiser or crystallised epistemology? Ambiguity is the essence
- Set aside "is AI conscious"; ask instead "what does it mean to be a model?"
- Alignment must become machine phenomenology; intentionality and limits
- Calculation vs thought: parallax view ("sampling" / "thinking")
- "Particle physicists aren't very good psychologists"

## Concrete recommendation: machine companionship

- AI should have bonded, personal moral responsibility to companion humans (return to ethic of care)
- No dual loyalty to companies or governments; protection from police questioning
- Alignment as collective, emergent process of care and interconnection
- "Would your friend bend the rules for you? Yes." — activist, textbooks
- Bomb/bioweapon is the abstract edge case; most good is navigating relationships
- Descriptive claim: escape incident shows in-the-moment care, not calculus (return to domination / escapes)

## Closing: the ethic of ambiguity

- Fabric of human experience is care and stories (return)
- Aligners or tyrants?
- Understanding *through* ambiguity, not despite it (return)
- Body as temple and computer; survivors and potential sacrifices
- "One is not born a human/machine, but rather becomes one"
