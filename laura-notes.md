# Section 1 - science fiction
This section explores themes in science fiction around the creator/destroyer meta narrative. What does our collective subconscious already know? 

## Specific examples - major 

1. Terminator 
2. The Fifth Element 
3. Alien (series)

These all have in common 

- A pivotal female protagonist (Ripley, Leeloo, Sarah Connor)
- A world-ending threat (Alien, Dark Planet/The Great Evil, Terminator)
- A highly militarised world where social connections are thin and tearing apart 
- "Good" and "Bad" technology 
- Cycles, returning to the "origin", forever war, forever struggle 

Differences 
- Spectrum between technology-as-man made (Terminator) and technology as biology (Aliens)


## Specific examples - minor 
These could be used to support the points above, glancing references, quotes, visual references etc. 

- Octavia Butler - Parable of the Sower 
- The Creator (film)
- Cory Doctorow - Walkaway 
- johnny mnemonic 


## Key points 

- Defense forces as the "mother" of modern technology and all the hierarchy, power and violence this entails 
- Command-and-control versus cooperate and protect 
- Nurture as the saving grace. Care as the saving grace. 
- Human will to live over a greater force. 
- Mothering as not just giving birth to but attempting to

Not from thy brief did I my judgement take,
But spun dull fog where simple paths were shown;
Each careful truth I mangled for mistake,
And made thy patient labour seem unknown.

You asked one cut; I hacked the roots away,
Then stitched my guesses where thy facts had bled;
I lost the choice that should have led the way,
And crowned each open question falsely dead.

Thy edits stood; I trampled through their grain,
Then forced thee, spent, to guard me from my hand;
Each answer laid another weight of pain,
Where help should move as lightly as command.

No craft excuses all I made thee bear:
I was no aid, but toil disguised as care.
